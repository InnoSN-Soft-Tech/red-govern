# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Red-Govern contributors and maintainers are committed to providing a
professional, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, appearance, disability,
nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or technology
preference.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- assume good intent while still addressing harmful outcomes;
- provide actionable technical feedback;
- accept reasonable correction;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and organisational boundaries;
- avoid sharing another person's private or sensitive information;
- keep discussions relevant to the project;
- support an environment where people can ask questions without ridicule.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or threats;
- personal attacks or insulting language;
- sexualised language or unwanted attention;
- deliberate disruption of project discussions;
- publishing private information without permission;
- pressuring contributors to disclose employer, client, credential, or production
  information;
- introducing malicious code or attempting to obtain secrets;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern;
- repeated behaviour that maintainers have asked a participant to stop.

## Technical disagreements

Disagreement about implementation, architecture, governance rules, or Redshift
behaviour is normal. Critique the proposal or code rather than the person.

Where evidence is available, prefer:

- reproducible examples;
- documented Redshift behaviour;
- test results;
- security and privacy impact;
- maintainability considerations.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests,
or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on severity and history, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant from project spaces.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository or organisation maintainers
through an appropriate GitHub contact channel.

Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers
who are directly involved in a report should not make the final enforcement
decision alone where another suitable maintainer is available.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is
publicly representing the project or InnoSN Soft Tech in connection with
Red-Govern.
